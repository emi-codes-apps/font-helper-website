Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Font Helper 🎨 (“Font Helper”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Font Helper Google Docs add-on/extension.

Plain-language summary: Font Helper is designed to use the minimum information needed to provide its features. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or intentionally store the contents of your Google Docs on a separate developer-operated server.

1. Information Font Helper may access

Depending on the features you use and the permissions granted through Google, Font Helper may access:

  • Google Docs content and formatting information needed to perform the action you request, such as text selections, font names, formatting, styles, or related document metadata.
  • Basic Google account information if Google makes it available as part of authentication or authorization.
  • Font Helper preferences or settings, which may be stored using Google Apps Script or Google-provided storage associated with your account.
  • Technical execution information generated by Google’s Apps Script platform, such as error or execution logs. Font Helper is not designed to intentionally place document contents into developer logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • provide the Font Helper features you request;
  • remember app preferences where appropriate;
  • diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve the extension; and
  • respond to support requests you send to us.

3. Storage and retention

Document content processed by Font Helper is intended to be handled within the Google Docs / Google Apps Script environment for the purpose of completing the requested action. We do not operate a separate database for storing your document contents.

User preferences may remain in Google-provided storage until they are replaced, cleared, or the add-on is removed. Emails that you send to support may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain support records.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be processed by Google as part of providing Google Docs, Google Apps Script, authentication, hosting, and related platform services. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Font Helper’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Permissions

Font Helper requests only the permissions required for its features. Google will show the permissions requested during authorization. You can review or revoke access at any time through your Google Account security settings.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to minimize access to user data and to keep Font Helper’s code and configuration secure. No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

Font Helper is a general productivity utility and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information outside what may be processed through Google services when the add-on is used.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Font Helper changes or when legal or platform requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to emi.codes.apps@gmail.com.
