Support

Support for Font Helper 🎨

For installation questions, permission problems, unexpected behaviour, or general help, contact the developer by email.

Email: emi.codes.apps@gmail.com

What to include

  • a short description of what you were trying to do;
  • the steps you took before the issue occurred;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • your browser and operating system, if relevant; and
  • a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.

Please avoid sending sensitive document content unless it is genuinely necessary for troubleshooting. Redact names, addresses, account numbers, or confidential material from screenshots where possible.
